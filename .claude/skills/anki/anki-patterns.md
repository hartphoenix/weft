# Anki Card Patterns

Learned preferences from skipped and edited cards. Read by the anki skill during card generation.

## Anti-patterns (cards that get skipped or edited)

- Check for duplicate cards from previous sessions before presenting — Aaron will ask you to check and it wastes time
- Cards covering concepts already in existing Mochi cards get skipped — always check previous session's cards before presenting

## Preferred style (learned from edits)

- Keep backs to 1-2 sentences max. Aaron actively trims longer backs ("remove third sentence", "shorten second sentence")
- Split multi-concept backs into separate cards ("split up the positive gradient part into another card")
- Use Aaron's own phrasing/framing when possible — his restatements are the card
- Front should ask the real question Aaron would ask, not the textbook version ("why do we use embeddings to represent tokens?" over "why can't you feed raw integers?")
